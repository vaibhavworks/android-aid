About the App

Android Aid helps developers and learners fix Android errors faster. With a searchable database of common issues, error codes, and solutions, it's your smart companion for Android development.

Key Features

  • 📱 Covers a wide range of Android errors – Organized and easy to navigate
  • 🔍 Instant Search – Quickly find errors by title or category
  • 📂 Smart Sorting – View errors grouped by category
  • 📝 Detailed Descriptions – Learn what the error means
  • 🔧 Troubleshooting Steps – Follow simple solutions to fix issues
  • 🌐 Offline Access – Use the app without an internet connection
  • 🎨 Simple & Clean UI – Designed for clarity and ease-of-use
